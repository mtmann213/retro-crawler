# Retro Crawler Release-Candidate Playtest

This checklist covers the outside-player gates that cannot be proven by automated tests. Start from a fresh save and do not coach the player unless they are completely blocked.

Record: player name, platform, controller or keyboard, total minutes, ending reached, rooms visited, optional cache found, boss attempts, and any point where the next action was unclear.

## Pass criteria

- The player leaves the Intake Shelter without help.
- The player understands that actions can spend dungeon-clock time.
- The player completes or intentionally extracts from the run in 15–30 minutes.
- The player can explain enemy intent before the second encounter ends.
- The player understands why the Warden changes phases.
- No crash, stuck focus, unreadable text, or save-loss issue occurs.
- At least 80% of outside players complete the slice without developer help.

## Questions after play

1. What were you trying to accomplish?
2. When did the clock feel important?
3. Did visible hostiles and locked routes make it clear when and why combat would begin?
4. Did the room props, route markings, and point-of-interest prompts make it clear where to walk and interact?
5. Was any text, sound, or animation distracting or hard to read?
6. Would you choose the cache detour again? Why?
